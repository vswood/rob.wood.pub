Executive
Summary

I work at the intersection of engineering execution, systems architecture, and organizational improvement.

I help organizations recover momentum when technology becomes a blocker.

That usually means stepping into environments where the technology is underperforming, the delivery process is noisy, or the business has outgrown the way the software was originally built.

I help teams regain control by clarifying the technical path, fixing structural problems, and improving the way software gets delivered.

Over the past 20+ years, I have modernized legacy products, rescued fragile systems, improved cloud foundations, and helped teams work with more speed and less noise.

My background spans hands-on engineering, architecture, platform work, and leadership, which makes me effective in situations where technical problems are tightly coupled to process and organizational drag.

My work has included stabilizing critical systems for companies such as Netflix and Sunrun, supporting nationally visible public-sector platforms, and helping turn internal tools into revenue-generating products.
